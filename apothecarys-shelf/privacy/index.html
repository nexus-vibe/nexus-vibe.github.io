Privacy policy

The Apothecary’s Shelf, a game by Nexus Vibe

The short version. The Apothecary’s Shelf does not collect anything about you. There are no accounts, no tracking, and no adverts. Your progress is saved on your own device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account. It is never sent to us.

What is stored, and where

The game saves which levels you have finished and their stars, the tinctures and recipes you have found, how many Step Backs, Bottles and Hints you hold, which letters you have read, and your sound and haptics settings. It is kept on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account, so it follows you to a new phone. We never receive any of it. Apple holds your iCloud data, not us.

What is not collected

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any identifier for your device. We do not use analytics. We do not use advertising. We cannot see how you play, and we do not know you have installed the game.

Deleting your data

Settings has a “Reset progress” button, which erases everything the game has saved, on this device and in your iCloud. Deleting the app removes it from the device; your iCloud copy stays until you reset progress or remove it in your iPhone’s iCloud settings.

Children

The game collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Purchases

If you buy something, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple’s own privacy policy covers that transaction.

Changes

If the game ever starts collecting anything — for example if adverts are added — this page will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here plainly.
