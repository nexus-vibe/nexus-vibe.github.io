Support

The Apothecary’s Shelf, a game by Nexus Vibe

Something gone wrong, or an idea to share? Write to studio@vibenexus.games and you will get a reply from a person.

It helps to include

What happened, what you expected instead, the level you were on if it was during one, your device and iOS version, and the version number from Settings, under About.

Lost progress

If you use iCloud, your progress comes back when you install the game on a new device signed in to the same account. Without iCloud it is kept only on the device, and cannot be recovered if the game is deleted or the device is lost.

Purchases and refunds

Purchases go through Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple rather than from us, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How long a reply takes

We read every message and reply as soon as we can.
